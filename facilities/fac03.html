Closed Return Aerodynamics Wind Tunnel

 

Specifications:

 

The Mechanical Engineering Department Closed Return Aerodynamics Wind Tunnel is a commercial closed return wind tunnel manufactured by Engineering Laboratory Design (ELD), Model 407A. The wind tunnel has an overall length of 34 ft and the test section has a cross section of 2 ft x 2 ft. The wind tunnel is powered by a 50 HP axial fan capable of delivering flow velocities as high as 55 m/s or 120 mph in the test section. The test section is equipped with a two axis force balance allowing measurement of lift and drag forces on the test article. Details of the Wind Tunnel can be found in the ELD website..
