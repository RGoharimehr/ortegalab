Low Speed Boundary Layer Wind Tunnel

 

Specifications:

 

The Low Speed Boundary Layer Wind Tunnel is a versatile open-return wind tunnel that has been used for many investigations. Flow is generated in a centrifugal blower equipped with backward facing vanes and is powered by a 5 hp Eaton Dynamatic magnetically clutched motor that provides outstanding speed control. Flow is conditioned by a series of honeycomb and SS screen panels, leading to a 8:1 contraction. The contraction leads to a test section measuring 8 ft (2.45 m) long, with a cross-section of 8 inches (20 cm) x 24 in (61 cm). Flow velocities up to 60 m/s with freestream turbulence of less than 1% have been achieved in this wind tunnel. The contraction and test section are designed to allow 90 degree rotation of the test section, thus either the 20 cm or 61 cm side can be oriented horizontally or vertically, depending on the application. Because of its open nature, different test sections are easily inserted downstream of the contraction. Recent investigations in this wind tunnel included studies of the flow and pressure drop in boundary layer flows with suction and flow visualization in air-cooled heat sinks.
