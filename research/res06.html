Optimization of a micro-channel automotive evaporator encompassing the use of Cu and its antimicrobial properties

Project Team


Alfonso Ortega and Timothy D. Guild

Project Sponsor


The International Copper Association

Objetive

 

Combine newly developed Copper micro-channel extrusion technology, flow distribution/manifold optimization, micro-channel placement/orientation, and world-class experimental testing coupled with numerical modeling to optimize an advanced heat exchanger operating with R134a in the 2-phase regime.

 

 

Process

 

 

1.  Design and build a test setup for data acquisition of the required parameters

2.  Test an industry benchmark Aluminum heat exchanger

3.  Design, fabricate, and test two bounding Copper heat exchangers

4.  Compare Copper heat exchanger results with the benchmark Aluminum results

5.  Develop numerical model to simulate parameter scaling of the heat exchanger variables necessary for optimization

6.  Separately experimentally test the Copper micro-channel tube and input the results into the Numerical model

7.  Use the Numerical model to appropriately size the heat exchanger for a specific application

8.  Review costs, materials, labor, feasibility, etc. involved with bringing the heat exchanger to market

9.  Deliver a competitive solution
