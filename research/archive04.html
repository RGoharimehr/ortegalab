The Development of Transient Compact Thermal Models

Project Team


Alfonso Ortega, J. Cole Smith, Colleen M. Gabel

Project Sponsor


AMD

Motivation

 

With the complete understanding of the Generation of Steady State Compact Thermal Models, we will now concentrate our efforts on developing a method to create Transient Compact Thermal Models. Presently, to find the junction temperature of an electronic package through the means of an FEM code, this can take many hours or days. At the close of this project, a methodology will be developed in which to represent a package with a small number of resistors and capacitors. By representing a large package with only a network of resistors and capacitors, the junction temperature will be computed in substantially less time.
 

Project Description

 
As an exact model, we will use an analytical solution to the transient fin equation. Currently, the 1-D equation with Neumann boundary conditions is being used to generate the tool. This transient CTM generator will be very similar to UACTM, which has been created for the steady state models. Once the method and transient CTM tool has been created and validated, the method will be modified for the 2-D and 3-D cases. PGA and BGA packages will be tested.
 

Questions to be Addressed:

 
What is the best optimization technique to find the capacitance values?
What will be the optimal network and will this be unique?
Should the steady-state resistance values be used in the generation of the the CTM?
