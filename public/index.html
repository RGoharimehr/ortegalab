Prospective Students

The LATFS lab at Villanova University is seeking motivated graduate students interested in experimental and computational research in thermal management, two-phase flow, and energy systems.

What We Offer

  • Fully-funded PhD and MS positions (tuition + stipend)
  • State-of-the-art research facilities
  • Collaboration with NSF E3S Center and industry partners
  • Strong publication record and conference travel support
  • Mentorship from experienced faculty and senior students

How to Apply

Send your CV, transcripts, and a brief research statement to the lab director. Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. Strong backgrounds in mechanical engineering, physics, or related fields are preferred.

Facilities

State-of-the-art equipment supporting cutting-edge research.

Two-Phase Flow & Boiling Lab

High-speed imaging systems, precision flow meters, and custom test sections for boiling and two-phase flow experiments.

  • Photron high-speed camera
  • Omega precision instruments
  • Custom heater assemblies

Thermal Characterization Suite

Advanced tools for measuring thermal resistance, conductivity, and transient thermal response of materials and systems.

  • IR thermography system
  • Laser flash diffusivity
  • Precision calorimetry

Computational Resources

High-performance computing cluster and licensed CFD software for large-scale simulations.

  • ANSYS Fluent & CFX
  • OpenFOAM cluster
  • MATLAB / Python toolchains

Microfluidics Lab

Cleanroom-class fabrication and testing of microchannels and heat spreaders for electronics cooling.

  • Soft lithography tools
  • Microscopy station
  • Micro-PIV system

Electronics Cooling Testbed

Dedicated infrastructure for testing advanced cooling solutions for high-power electronics.

  • Power electronics test boards
  • Junction temperature monitors
  • Custom cold plates

Energy Systems Lab

Research into sustainable energy conversion, heat exchangers, and thermal energy storage systems.

  • Heat exchanger test rigs
  • PCM storage modules
  • Solar thermal collectors
