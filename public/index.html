Join Our Lab

We are seeking talented graduate students and postdoctoral researchers.

Open Positions

PhD
Graduate Research Assistant — Thermal Management

Fully-funded PhD position in experimental two-phase heat transfer and thermal management of electronics. Start date: Fall 2025.

MS
Graduate Research Assistant — Energy Systems

Funded MS position in computational modeling of heat exchangers and energy conversion systems.

What We Offer

  • Fully-funded PhD and MS positions (tuition + competitive stipend)
  • State-of-the-art experimental and computational facilities
  • Collaboration with NSF E3S Center and industry partners
  • Strong publication record and conference travel support
  • Mentorship and professional development opportunities

How to Apply

Send the following materials to the lab director at aortega@villanova.edu:

  1. Your CV or resume
  2. Unofficial transcripts
  3. A brief (1–2 paragraph) research statement
  4. Contact information for 2–3 references

Applications are reviewed on a rolling basis. Backgrounds in mechanical engineering, physics, or related fields are preferred.

Frequently Asked Questions

Are funding packages guaranteed?

Yes. PhD and MS positions in our lab come with full tuition coverage and a monthly stipend for the duration of your appointment, subject to satisfactory academic progress.

What backgrounds are preferred?

We welcome students with backgrounds in mechanical engineering, chemical engineering, physics, or applied mathematics. Strong fundamentals in heat transfer, fluid mechanics, or thermodynamics are a plus.

Can international students apply?

Absolutely. We have a diverse lab with students from around the world. Villanova University supports international students through the Office of International Students.

How competitive is the application process?

We admit a small number of students each year to maintain a mentored environment. We prioritize research fit, academic preparation, and motivation.
