Graduate Opportunities

LATFS welcomes applications from highly motivated graduate students with backgrounds in mechanical engineering, physics, or related fields who are interested in pursuing research in thermal and fluid sciences.

Research Areas for Graduate Students:

  • •Thermal management of electronic systems
  • •Mini and microchannel heat exchangers
  • •Droplet impingement and spray cooling
  • •Energy technology and geothermal systems
  • •Computational fluid dynamics (CFD)

Interested candidates should contact Dr. Ortega directly with their CV and a brief statement of research interests.

Undergraduate Opportunities

Undergraduate students have opportunities to participate in LATFS research through independent study, senior thesis projects, and the NSF REU (Research Experiences for Undergraduates) program.

Benefits of Undergraduate Research:

  • •Hands-on laboratory experience
  • •Mentorship from faculty and graduate students
  • •Potential for co-authorship on publications
  • •Preparation for graduate school applications
  • •Conference presentation opportunities

Interested students should be enrolled in ME, EE, or related programs at Villanova. Prior coursework in thermodynamics or fluid mechanics preferred.

Facilities

State-of-the-art research equipment and laboratory infrastructure

Thermal Imaging Systems

Liquid crystal transient thermal imaging systems for high-resolution surface temperature measurement. Infrared cameras for non-contact thermal measurement.

High-Speed Video Imaging

High-speed digital cameras for capturing fast transient phenomena such as droplet impingement, spray dynamics, and fluid instabilities.

Spray & Droplet Test Rigs

Custom-built experimental setups for droplet impingement studies, spray cooling experiments, and two-phase flow characterization.

Microchannel Test Facilities

Precision flow and pressure measurement systems for characterizing mini and microchannel heat sink performance in single and two-phase flow regimes.

Computational Resources

High-performance computing cluster for CFD simulations using FLUENT, OpenFOAM and custom numerical codes. Workstations for data analysis.

Data Acquisition Systems

National Instruments DAQ systems, thermocouples, pressure transducers, and flow meters for comprehensive experimental data collection.
